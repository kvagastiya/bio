01 / EXCRETION
FLICKERING CILIA

Specialised Flame Cells

Specialised cells called flame cells help in osmoregulation and excretion, using beating ciliary tufts to propel excess water and nitrogenous waste into excretory ducts.

PROTONEPHRIDIA • WATER & ION BALANCE
02 / LIFE CYCLE MONOECIOUS ⚥

Hermaphrodite & Larval Stages

Sexes are not separate (hermaphroditic). Fertilisation is internal and indirect development is through many larval stages across one or more hosts.

INTERNAL FERTILISATION • MULTI-LARVAL DEVELOPMENT
03 / REGENERATION PLANARIA

High Regeneration Capacity

Some members like Planaria possess high regeneration capacity, regrowing complete organisms from cut fragments.

CLASS TREMATODA • ENDOPARASITE

Fasciola
(Liver Fluke)

Dorso-ventrally flattened leaf-shaped parasite infecting the liver and bile ducts of vertebrates; attaches via muscular oral and ventral suckers.

HOST: LIVER & BILE DUCTS
